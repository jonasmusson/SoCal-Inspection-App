# Real-world inspection test

Use this for the first shop-floor inspection run. Treat the run as validation, not customer delivery.

## Before starting
- Manager signs in on desktop and creates/checks in a test inspection.
- Assign the inspection to the technician who will perform the test.
- Tech signs in on the phone and confirms the assigned vehicle appears under My Inspections.
- Use a real vehicle, but do not send the finished report to a customer during this test.

## During the inspection
- Confirm Start Inspection opens the first incomplete section.
- Record at least one Good, Monitor, Needs Attention, and Not Inspected finding.
- For Monitor/Needs Attention, enter observation, why it matters, recommended action, and any useful labor/parts planning ranges.
- Capture at least one photo and one video from the phone.
- Exercise any item configured with required notes/photo/video and confirm the section cannot complete until the required evidence is present.
- Pause and resume the timer once from the inspection controls.
- Use Save & Exit once, return to the inspection, and confirm the timer remains paused until explicitly resumed.
- Complete multiple sections and confirm progress advances and previously entered findings remain saved.
- Note any confusing wording, extra taps, slow media behavior, or anything a technician is tempted to skip.

## Manager review
- Complete the final section and confirm status becomes Pending Review.
- Manager opens Review & Approve and verifies findings, photos, priorities, notes, and planning ranges.
- Preview the premium report and confirm vehicle/customer details and evidence are correct.
- Print / Save PDF and inspect page breaks, image sizing, and readability.
- Do not use customer Send/Resend until the test record and recipient are intentionally safe.

## Timer reality check
Compare displayed labor time with the approximate wall-clock test time, subtracting the intentional paused period. Record any unexplained difference. Pay special attention to navigation away from an active section and phone background/lock behavior.

## What counts as a successful first field test
- Correct tech can access only the assigned inspection path intended for them.
- Findings persist after navigation and reload.
- Required-field/media gates behave correctly.
- Photo and video uploads succeed from the phone.
- Pause/resume and Save & Exit do not inflate labor time.
- Final section moves the inspection to Pending Review.
- Manager can review and generate the report with the captured evidence.
- No customer report is accidentally sent during validation.

Record every friction point before changing workflow assumptions; the purpose of this run is to learn how a technician actually uses the app in the shop.
